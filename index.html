Three ways to use it. Zero servers.

Everything happens on the chip inside your phone, the same way your camera processes a photo.

Chat

Ask questions, get homework explained, brainstorm ideas or debug code. Replies stream in word by word, right on the device.

Voice

Hold to talk. Lantern turns your speech into text on the phone, thinks, and reads the answer back out loud.

Camera

Snap a photo of a circuit, a plant or a page of notes and ask about it. The image is analysed without being uploaded anywhere.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Download Lantern on your Android phone. No sign‑up, no email, no account.

  2. 2

    Download a brain, once

    Pick a model sized for your phone. It downloads one time over Wi‑Fi and is stored on the device.

  3. 3

    Use it anywhere

    On a plane, in the hills, or with flight mode on. The AI keeps working because it never needed the internet.

Bigger, not faster

Passing work between devices over Wi‑Fi adds delay. The cluster lets you run smarter models; small models stay quickest on one device.

Questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. You need internet only to install the app and download a model the first time. After that, every answer is generated on your phone.

Is anything I type or say sent somewhere?

No. Your messages, voice and photos are processed on the device. There is no server to send them to.

Is it as smart as cloud AI?

Not quite. Phone‑sized models are great for explanations, writing help and everyday questions, but giant cloud models still know more and reason better on very hard problems.

Can the TV join a cluster?

The TV can display a cluster’s chat, but it can’t lend memory. Its small amount of RAM is already used by the TV system, and TV apps can’t run AI models.

Will it drain my battery?

Generating answers works the processor hard, a bit like gaming, so long sessions use noticeable battery. Short questions barely make a dent.
